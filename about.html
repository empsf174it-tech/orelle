The House of Orelle

Founded on structure, depth, and restraint, Orelle composes fragrances for those who wear their presence boldly.

The Architecture of Scent

Orelle was founded on a singular principle: fragrance should be structured, profound, and unapologetically bold.

We discard the ephemeral and the overly sweet, focusing instead on deep woods, rare resins, structured florals, and dark spices. Every bottle is an exercise in tension and release, crafted by master perfumers utilizing the finest raw materials sourced globally.

Authenticity & Craftsmanship

Every batch is macerated for 6 months to ensure ultimate depth.

FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to what our clients ask most. For anything else, follow the house or send us an inquiry.

Instagram X Facebook Pinterest WhatsApp
How long does shipping take?

Orders ship within 1–2 business days. Standard delivery takes 3–5 business days; express arrives in 1–2. Every order is gift-wrapped at no extra cost.

What is your return policy?

Unopened bottles may be returned within 30 days of delivery. Each full-size order includes a sample vial so you can try the scent before breaking the seal.

How long do Orelle fragrances last?

Our extraits are macerated for six months and typically last 8–12 hours on skin, longer on fabric. Longevity varies with skin chemistry and climate.

Can I book a private consultation?

Yes. Choose "VIP Concierge" in the form above, or visit the Place Vendôme flagship by appointment for a one-to-one scent profiling session.

Do you offer custom engraving or packaging?

Personalised engraving and bespoke packaging are available through the Studio.
