Shipping & Returns

Policies crafted for a seamless experience.

Shipping Policy

Complimentary standard shipping is provided on all orders within the contiguous United States. Expedited shipping is available at checkout.

Fragrance Shipping Restrictions

Due to alcohol content, fragrances are considered hazardous materials and must be shipped via ground transport. We cannot ship to P.O. Boxes or international destinations at this time.

Returns & Exchanges

We accept returns within 30 days of delivery. Items must be unopened and in their original packaging. Because every full-size bottle includes a complimentary sample, we ask that you test the sample first before breaking the seal on the full bottle.

Custom engraved items and Discovery Sets are final sale and cannot be returned.
